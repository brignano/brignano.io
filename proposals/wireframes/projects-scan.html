Choices behind it

  • Colour comes from each product's own mark. Sites magenta, Guidebook violet, Driftwood teal, the same colours their product pages use. The other three use the family amber, since the design rules give a product its own colour only once it has a product page. The pictures still tell them apart.
  • Drawn pictures, not screenshots. Screenshots go stale and are unreadable at card size on a phone. Drawings stay sharp, follow light and dark, and show the one idea. If you'd rather have real screenshots, they slot into the same space.
  • Status says "live" in green only when it is. Beta goes neutral, so blue keeps meaning "you can click this", as the design system asks.
  • Details opens in place, using the design system's existing disclosure pattern, so nothing new is needed in brignano/design. Open cards are remembered only for the visit.
  • The "how the work gets made" group shrinks to rows with a small glyph and one line. It's for engineers, so it shouldn't compete with the products.

Considered and dropped: filter chips (nine items don't need filtering); a separate page per project (more to maintain, and the Details panel covers it); one card per row on desktop (twice the scrolling).
