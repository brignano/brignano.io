Wireframe · /resume

Make the resume easy to digest

Same content as today and the PDF, nothing cut. What changes is the order you meet it in: one button and three quiet links at the top, the summary as the opening paragraph, each role readable before you open it, and the long lists (skills, leadership, education) as tight rows instead of stacks of cards.

What makes it hard to read today

10.9 screens

on a phone (9,192px at 390px wide). The Skills section alone is 2,000px: nine cards, each a heading over a cloud of pills.

4 equal buttons

View PDF, Share, LinkedIn and GitHub are all button-sized, so on a phone GitHub wraps onto its own line and nothing says which one matters.

3-line title

"Senior Staff Software Engineer — Enterprise Platform Engineering" at 24px wraps to three big lines, then "Summary" is a 30px heading for one paragraph.

5 closed roles

show only a date and a title. You have to open each one to learn what the job was, even though every role already has a one-sentence summary.

Proposed page

On a phone the page goes from 10.9 screens to about 5.8. Tap Show all on a role, or More on a leadership row, to see what sits behind it. On desktop the name, the PDF button and a short "on this page" list stay pinned on the left while the resume scrolls on the right.

Choices behind it

  • One button, three links. View PDF stays the blue button. Share, LinkedIn and GitHub become text links on the same line, the way the projects page now does it. At 390px all four fit on one row; in the desktop sidebar the button sits full width with the three links on one line under it.
  • The title splits at the dash. "Senior Staff Software Engineer" on one line, "Enterprise Platform Engineering · The Hartford" smaller beneath it. Role cards split the same way, so long titles stop wrapping to three lines.
  • No "Summary" heading. The summary becomes the opening paragraph under the links, at body size.
  • Roles say what they were while closed. Older roles show their existing one-sentence summary; the current role shows its first three highlights. "Show all" opens the rest and the tech list. Every bullet in the PDF is still one tap away.
  • Lists become rows. Skills turn into nine labelled lines of plain text. Leadership and education become rows with dividers. Projects shrink to a name and one line, with "All projects" linking to /projects, since the PDF leaves projects out anyway.
  • Smaller section headings (22–24px, down from 30px), matching home and projects.
  • Wording is unchanged. Every sentence comes from public/resume.yml as it stands, which already follows your Nov 3 career-highlights PDF. The PDF itself doesn't change.

Considered and dropped: a row of headline numbers under the hero (it would repeat what the summary and first role already say, the same repetition we cut from home); tabs for each section (hides content from search and Cmd+F); icon-only LinkedIn and GitHub buttons (shorter, but unlabelled icons were removed from this page before for being unclear).
