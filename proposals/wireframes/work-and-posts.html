Wireframe · /work + /blog

One page for the case studies and the posts

Today /work has three case studies and /blog has one post, and both read like long write-ups. This folds them into one list. Each entry has a small tag saying what it is, and a filter at the top lets a recruiter see just the case studies.

What to call it

Round two. "Work" and "Blog" are out: Work reads as a third professional page next to Projects and Resume, and Blog buries the case studies. These names say "things I wrote down while doing the work", which covers both kinds. Use the Name switch above the frames to see each one in place. The page sits last in the nav, after the professional pages.

Field notes

Notes from doing the work, at the job or at home. It fits a migration write-up and a homelab post equally, and feels like you rather than a section of a CV. Address: /notes.

Notes Chosen for now

The plain version of the same idea. Andy Matuschak calls his public notes "working notes". Safe, a little generic.

Logbook

Matches how you already work (the homelab's setup log). Charming, but it hints at short dated entries more than long write-ups. Simon Willison's "TIL" is the same flavour.

Journal

Warm and personal, but it can read as a diary, which undersells the case studies.

Proposed page

Try the filter inside either frame. The three faded entries are the posts being drafted in the other thread, with working titles, so you can see the page once they land; they only appear on the live site after you approve each post.

Choices behind it

  • A list, not cards. Case studies and posts are both things you read, so they share one quiet list like today's blog page. The big case-study cards stay on the home page, where they sell.
  • Case studies first under "All". They have no dates, and they're what a recruiter came for. Posts follow, newest first, with date and reading time.
  • The filter is three text tabs with counts, the same weight as the text links on /projects. Picking one updates the address (/notes?show=case-studies), so you can send a recruiter a link that opens on the case studies alone.
  • The tag is the only colour. "Case study" gets the interactive blue outline, "Post" stays grey. No icons or pictures.
  • The nav drops to four links, with this page last, after the professional ones. The home page's "Work" section keeps its name and its cards link to the case studies at their new address.
  • RSS stays at /rss.xml and still lists posts only, linking to their new addresses.

Considered and dropped: two sections on one page (that's the two pages stacked, the overlap stays); a dropdown filter (hides the choice); putting case studies in the RSS feed (they rarely change, and feed readers expect posts).

Old addresses keep working

Permanent redirects, so links already shared, search results and RSS readers all land in the right place. Shown for "Notes" at /notes; with another name, only the new side changes.
