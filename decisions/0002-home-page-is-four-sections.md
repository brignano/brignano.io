# ADR-0002: The home page is four sections

**Status:** accepted · 2026-11-15 · PR #339 · wireframe `proposals/wireframes/home-digest.html`

## Context

The home page had nine sections, repeated the same headline number six times
and ran about twelve screens on a phone.

## Decision

Home has four sections: the hero, three work cards (one number each, taken
from the case studies), the three featured side projects, and a sign-off with
one photo. Everything else that used to be there has a home of its own:
the role and highlights on /resume, case studies on /notes, activity on
/coding.

## Consequences

New content goes to the page it belongs to, not to home. Adding a fifth
section to home is a new decision with its own wireframe.
